# Profiles

### 9 year old Trayvon - Student

Trayvon is the youngest in a 5 person household. He was given an iPad fairly young and has become very tech literate. Trayvon has a very short attention span, and its quite hard for him to stick to one app at a time. He plays many different games, many involving some form of socialization such as Roblox or Among Us. In fact, Trayvon often plays Among Us on his off time and has a great sense when it comes to hidden role games.

Trayvon doesn't know anything aboout Bitten Ball or 'Jackbox' types games, and is coming in blind to the experience.


### 23 year old Rebecca - Student

Rebecca is on her last legs of the semester studying fashion design. She has a group of art friends she hangs out with through discord, often playing Roblox or some other games. Tonight for game night, the group decided to play another game of Bitten Ball.

Rebecca doesn't use her phone as much when playing this, she's just on her laptop in her bed. During the session, the camera is required to stay on in the call, but Rebecca's camera is fairly dated. 

Rebecca is somewhat an avid gamer, but her gaming experience is fairly insular. 

### 42 year old Robert - CEO

Considers himself 'entrepreneur'
Used his father money to start a tech company whose entire pitch is "ai for dogs"
Manages a small team of 10 employees, all of which don't like him much

Robert does not play games, his employees simply suggested to try Bitten Ball during the work party. Robert has a very expensive phone, but he's not very agile with it. He is in fact fairly tech illiterate.
